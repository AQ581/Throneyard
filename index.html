Monthly honors

Top your city. Rule your continent. Beat the world.

Every month the best players earn a badge that stays on their profile for good, and get featured at the top of Throneyard and in the Discord. Open to Throne Pass members in every country.

City Champion

Finish the month #1 in your city.

Continental Champion

Finish the month #1 on your continent: North America, Europe, Asia, South America, Africa or Oceania.

World Champion

After each month, every country's best player battles in the World Final (at least 4 finalists). Highest verified score is crowned World Champion.

Badges are honors only, with no cash or prizes. Throne Pass members earn them, decided by verified scores. How badges work.
